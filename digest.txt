🎙 Podcast

Extreme Harness Engineering for Token Billionaires: 1M LOC, 1B toks/day, 0% human code, 0% human review — Ryan Lopopolo, OpenAI Frontier &amp; Symphony

在这期 Latent Space 节目中，OpenAI Frontier 团队的 Ryan Lopopolo 透露，他的团队在五个月内构建了一个超过百万行代码的代码库，全程没有人工编写一行代码，开发效率约为传统工程方式的十倍。Lopopolo 通过 Codex CLI 完全以 AI 代理驱动开发，以此验证 AI 智能体能够完全替代工程师工作输出的核心论点。一个尤为值得关注的细节是：当新版模型引入后台 Shell 功能后，模型对缓慢的构建过程变得缺乏耐心，迫使团队在约一周内将整个构建系统从 Make 迁移至 Bazel、再到 Turbo，最终落定 NX，使构建时间压缩至一分钟以内。Lopopolo 将"harness 工程"——即围绕 AI 编程智能体设计脚手架、工具链和提示词环境的专业能力——定位为一门新兴独立学科，其核心在于系统性地识别智能体的失误点，并逐步建立对各自动化环节的信心。

In this Latent Space episode, Ryan Lopopolo from OpenAI's Frontier team reveals that his team built a million-line codebase over five months without writing a single line of code themselves, achieving roughly 10x faster development than a traditional engineering approach. Working exclusively through the Codex CLI harness, Lopopolo imposed a self-constraint of zero human-written code to prove that AI agents could fully replicate an engineer's output — a thesis he now considers validated. A particularly striking operational detail emerged around model generations: when GPT-4.1 (o3/codex) introduced background shell capabilities, the model became less patient with slow builds, forcing the team to overhaul their entire build system to complete in under one minute, cycling through Make, Bazel, Turbo, and finally NX in roughly a week. Lopopolo frames "harness engineering" — the discipline of designing the scaffolding, tooling, and prompting environment around AI coding agents — as an emerging, distinct craft requiring systems-thinking to identify where agents fail and how to build confidence in each automated layer of the SDLC.
