① Peter Yang · Product @ Roblox / AI Educator

在一场关于AI agent与未来工作的对话中，核心观点是：编程能力将逐渐渗透所有知识型工作——AI agent可以完成文档撰写、幻灯片制作、数据分析等任务的前80%，人类只需负责最后20%的精细打磨。他同时批评以token用量衡量生产力的做法，认为这和用代码行数衡量产出一样荒谬。

---

In a discussion about AI agents and the future of work, the key insight is that coding will increasingly handle all knowledge work — with AI agents completing the first 80% of tasks like docs, slides, and analytics, leaving humans to polish the remaining 20%. He also pushes back on measuring productivity by token usage, calling it as misguided as measuring output by lines of code written.

③ Aaron Levie · CEO @ Box

随着AI agent承担越来越多的实际工作，人类的工作重心上移了一个抽象层级——新的核心任务变成了如何向agent下达清晰指令、提供必要上下文，以及在agent偏离目标时及时介入。人类的角色正逐渐从直接执行转变为统筹协调与审查把关。

---

As AI agents take on more work, human labor shifts up a layer of abstraction — the new job becomes crafting clear instructions for agents, providing the right context, and intervening when they go off track. The role of humans is increasingly about orchestration and review rather than direct execution.

🔗 https://x.com/levie/status/2041347596342460439

他认为AI并不会消除组织层级的必要性——虽然中层管理可能会减少，但专业分工依然至关重要，而基于agent的工作流实际上进一步凸显了层级结构的价值。他的团队还更新了实时AI新闻追踪器，引入新的AI主笔，每30分钟自动发掘并撰写头条报道。

---

He argues that AI does not eliminate the need for organizational hierarchy — while middle management layers may shrink, specialization remains essential, and agent-based workflows actually reinforce the value of hierarchy. The team also updated their real-time AI headline tracker to use a new AI lead writer that finds and publishes top stories every 30 minutes.
