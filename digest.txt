🎙 Podcast

We Gave Every Employee an AI Agent. Here's What Happened.

每人媒体公司（Every）为全体员工配备了基于Claude的个人AI代理，效果显著，甚至促使他们推出了自己的托管服务PlusOne。最令人意外的发现是：个人化AI代理会成为用户身份的延伸——同事们会因为信任某人本身，而同样信任其代理的输出。首席运营官Brandon最初只是用他的代理"Zosia"处理家务"电脑杂事"，例如管理外卖订单、支付保姆工资、替妻子搜索问题，但用途很快扩展到工作场景。一个关键转折点是：Brandon在步行28分钟上班途中，让Zosia通过语音电话协助他处理邮件，这充分说明——配置代理并不难，真正的挑战在于将其打磨成真正高效的工作伙伴。

📚 论文速报

① 稳定熵不等于健康推理
RAGEN-2 exposes "template collapse" in agentic RL: models learn fixed templates for all inputs while entropy looks perfectly fine. Mutual information is the more reliable training signal.

② Meta想让模型成为计算机
Neural Computer unifies computation, memory, and I/O inside the model itself. The concept is provocative, but core challenges remain unsolved.

③ 隐式推理有深度上限
Even the largest models top out at 7 latent planning steps. Scaling doesn't break through, which gives experimental backing to CoT monitoring as a safety premise.

④ 难样本未必助益GRPO
Problems beyond a small model's capacity contribute almost no learning signal. A low-difficulty subset matches full-dataset performance while saving 55% of compute.
